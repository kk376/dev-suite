# Anti-Slop Copywriting & Technical Prose Standard

A rigorous standard for writing human, clear, and authoritative technical documentation, product copy, interface text, and engineering communication. Eliminates recognizable AI writing tells, rejects hollow buzzwords, enforces factual integrity, and purges syntactic clichés.

---

## 1. Core Principles of Technical Copywriting

1. **Zero Hallucinated Specifics**: Never invent metrics, customer names, dates, quotes, or regulatory claims. If a sentence requires concrete data to be effective, obtain primary source figures or write the direct, unadorned version.
2. **Elimination of Punctuation Dashes**: The em dash character, en dash, and double hyphens used as punctuation are strictly prohibited in agent-authored copy. Use commas, colons, parentheses, or periods to separate clauses.
3. **Active Human Voice**: Speak directly as an engineer or product creator. Do not hide behind corporate abstractions, passive grammar, or artificial modesty.
4. **Resist Over-Sterilization**: Removing machine tells does not mean stripping away clarity or character. Retain intentional voice and domain accuracy.

---

## 2. The 12 AI Writing Tells & Refactoring Protocol

### 1. Empty AI Vocabulary
- **The Pattern**: Stacking abstract buzzwords to manufacture importance: *unlock, elevate, empower, delve, showcase, testament, landscape, journey, robust, game-changer, seamless, cutting-edge, revolutionary*.
- **Why It Reads as AI**: These words appear with high frequency in model-generated text. They signal intent to impress rather than inform.
- **Before**: *Unlock the power of seamless collaboration to elevate your engineering journey to the next level.*
- **After**: *Collaborate with your team in a single shared repository.*

### 2. Significance Inflation
- **The Pattern**: Declaring ordinary tools as monumental breakthroughs: *the future of X*, *marking a pivotal moment*, *ushering in a new era*, *a testament to*.
- **Why It Reads as AI**: Grandiose phrasing masks a lack of concrete utility.
- **Before**: *Our cache layer marks a pivotal moment in backend performance, ushering in a new era of data delivery.*
- **After**: *Our cache layer reduces database read queries by seventy percent.*

### 3. Empty Claims & Unverified Social Proof
- **The Pattern**: Citing vague authority or synthetic popularity: *Trusted by thousands of developers*, *industry-leading*, *loved by teams everywhere*.
- **Why It Reads as AI**: Unsubstantiated claims are immediately recognized as synthetic marketing filler.
- **Before**: *Trusted by top engineering teams worldwide for mission-critical reliability.*
- **After**: *Used in production by named organizations, or omitted entirely if no external customers exist.*

### 4. Weasel Attributions
- **The Pattern**: Hiding unsourced claims behind anonymous consensus: *Experts agree*, *industry observers report*, *leading analysts suggest*.
- **Why It Reads as AI**: Artificial authority created to bypass citation requirements.
- **Before**: *Industry observers note that moving to serverless dramatically improves developer velocity.*
- **After**: *Teams deploying to serverless reduce infrastructure configuration overhead, or cite the specific benchmark report.*

### 5. Persuasive Authority Tropes
- **The Pattern**: Grand rhetorical setups pretending to reveal hidden truths: *at its core*, *the real question is*, *what really matters*, *fundamentally*, *the heart of the matter*.
- **Why It Reads as AI**: Theatrical pauses that delay delivering actual technical points.
- **Before**: *At its core, what really matters in distributed systems is whether nodes can reach consensus.*
- **After**: *Distributed systems depend on whether nodes can reach consensus before the network timeout expires.*

### 6. Chatbot Closers
- **The Pattern**: Conversational artifacts appended to deliverable content: *I hope this helps!*, *Let me know if you have questions!*, *Feel free to reach out.*
- **Why It Reads as AI**: Residual artifacts from chat completions pasted into technical deliverables.
- **Before**: *Here is the updated schema migration. I hope this helps! Let me know if you need anything else.*
- **After**: *The updated schema migration adds the required foreign key constraint with zero table locks.*

### 7. Fake-Candid Openers
- **The Pattern**: Feigned personal intimacy: *Honestly?*, *Let's be honest*, *Here's the thing*, *Real talk*.
- **Why It Reads as AI**: Manufactured colloquialism that sounds scripted when generated by an assistant.
- **Before**: *Here's the thing: microservices are often unnecessary for early-stage architectures.*
- **After**: *Monolithic architectures reduce operational complexity for early-stage teams.*

### 8. Signposting Announcements
- **The Pattern**: Narrating the act of writing rather than stating the content: *Let's dive in*, *Here's what you need to know*, *In this section we will explore*, *Without further ado*.
- **Why It Reads as AI**: Meta-commentary that wastes reading time and mimics tutorial templates.
- **Before**: *Without further ado, let us dive into how connection pooling functions in PostgreSQL.*
- **After**: *PostgreSQL connection pooling reuses established backend processes to prevent connection exhaustion.*

### 9. All-Caps Emphasis
- **The Pattern**: Shouting entire words or clauses in uppercase within paragraphs to simulate urgency.
- **Why It Reads as AI**: A blunt mechanism used when sentence rhythm fails to establish proper priority.
- **Before**: *The database primary must be protected and YOU MUST NEVER run unindexed queries in production.*
- **After**: *The primary database must be protected. Unindexed queries in production will exhaust memory and cause downtime.*

### 10. Actorless Passive Voice
- **The Pattern**: Eliminating the responsible party from operational actions: *the decision was made to drop support*, *the endpoint was deprecated*.
- **Why It Reads as AI**: Models often lack context regarding who made a decision, so they delete the actor entirely.
- **Before**: *The decision was made to deprecate the v1 REST API.*
- **After**: *We deprecated the v1 REST API in favor of the gRPC interface.*

### 11. Inanimate Subject with Human Agency
- **The Pattern**: Assigning human thoughts and emotions to software abstractions: *the compiler understands your code*, *the dashboard knows what you need*, *the framework wants you to*.
- **Why It Reads as AI**: Flattering software with human consciousness instead of describing operational behavior.
- **Before**: *The caching layer understands user access patterns and intelligently surfaces content.*
- **After**: *The caching layer stores frequently requested records using a least-recently-used eviction policy.*

### 12. Negative Parallelism & Tacked-On Negations
- **The Pattern**: Overusing rhetorical dualisms: *It is not just X, it is Y*, *Not only A, but also B*, or appending clipped fragments such as *no guessing*, *no wasted motion*.
- **Why It Reads as AI**: A formulaic rhetorical trick used to manufacture emphasis out of thin air.
- **Before**: *This is not just a logging tool, it is a unified observability engine. All telemetry in one place, no guessing.*
- **After**: *This engine unifies distributed traces, application logs, and system metrics in a single interface.*

## 3. Structural & Rhetorical Discipline

### The Rule of Three Overuse
Avoid forcing every group of features or concepts into triplets (such as *speed, simplicity, and scale*). Real engineering systems have as many components as the domain requires: two, four, or seven. State the exact list dictated by reality.

### Aphorism Formulas
Eliminate faux-philosophical templates:
- *X is the currency of Y* (e.g. *Latency is the currency of modern web applications*)
- *X is not just a tool, it is a philosophy*
- *In a world where X, Y becomes paramount*

Replace them with direct engineering statements: *High network latency directly reduces user session duration.*

## 4. Copywriting Verification Checklist

Execute this checklist before delivering any documentation, UI copy, or written technical content:

```markdown
### Anti-Slop Prose & Copywriting Verification

- [x] Zero Em Dashes: Verified no em dashes, en dashes, or punctuation hyphens in text.
- [x] Zero Buzzword Soup: Verified absence of elevate, empower, seamless, revolutionary.
- [x] Verified Facts Only: Zero fabricated numbers, user counts, or customer quotes.
- [x] Direct Subject Openers: Zero meta-signposting (dive in, without further ado).
- [x] Concrete Utility: Significance inflation removed; features described by mechanism.
- [x] Active Engineering Voice: Actor present in statements; software abstractions not personified.
- [x] Clean Deliverable Finish: Zero residual chatbot pleasantries or closers.
- [x] Natural List Cadence: Lists reflect actual items rather than forced triplets.
```
